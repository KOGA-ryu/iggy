The user authorized these three next families after trying P041. The native
`math_lab` now offers thirteen objects. Each new family has four working levels

## Controls and mathematical conventions

Modular arithmetic uses canonical residues `0 <= r < n`, including negative
integers. Moduli range from 2 to 12. Forward/backward steps change the current
residue and observed-visit mask, capped at 64 steps; changing modulus, step or
integer resets that evidence. Reset walk returns to zero. Cycle length is
`n/gcd(n,k)` even for zero steps. Inverses exist precisely for coprime
multipliers. The CRT view supports noncoprime moduli and incompatible residues;
it reports consistency, the LCM period and the least nonnegative solution when
one exists. Two drums and stem plots display the simultaneous conditions.

Gaussian inputs are integer pairs: z has coordinates -3..3 and w has -2..2.
The norm is `a*a+b*b`. The optional height is `0.12*N(z)`; the axes remain in
the complex plane, and a uniform presentation scale keeps the shown points in
view. Euclidean division rounds each quotient coordinate to its nearest
integer (ties away from zero), giving `z=q*w+r` and `N(r)<N(w)` for nonzero w.
Division by zero is explicitly undefined; the ideal generated by zero contains
only zero. Gold lattice points are ideal members.

Quotient presets are Z[i]/(2), Z[i]/(2+i), and Z[i]/(3), with 4, 5 and 9
classes. Canonical representatives use floor coordinates in the half-open
parallelogram spanned by the generator and i times the generator; this differs
from the nearest-integer division remainder. Class colours and the table share
those representatives. Units and nonzero zero divisors are computed by exact
finite multiplication. In Z[i]/(2), the nonzero class of 1+i squares to zero.
The other two presets are fields. Their characteristics are 2, 5 and 3.

The chamber offers three smooth fields on all of R^3: constant (1,.5,-.25),
radial (x,y,z), and vortex (-y,x,0). Paths are a straight segment, upper/lower
semicircles, a nonplanar arch and a closed circle. The orange field arrow has
common scale .3; the gold unit direction/tangent has scale .65. Path time lies
in [0,1]. Reverse path keeps the world probe in place, reverses its tangent,
and pauses. Play, Pause, Advance, Restart and plot scrubbing share the existing
semantic time route and stop at the endpoint.

Line integrals use analytic velocities and a 256-panel Simpson rule in double
precision. Constant/radial fields also expose exact potential differences;
the vortex has curl (0,0,2). Level 3 compares with the straight route between
the same endpoints, or the stationary route for a closed loop. Reversing the
unit circle changes vortex circulation from 2*pi to -2*pi. Coordinate, work-rate
and accumulated-work plots follow the same probe state. Tables and plots use
tabs when both are present.

## Ownership and bounds

The existing MathObjects model owns all mathematical truth, challenge checks
and actions. The UI presents a new bounded 32-row/four-value-column snapshot
table and sends semantic actions. Labels increase from 16 to 32. Primitive,
plot, vertex and index capacities are unchanged; no scene adapter, renderer,
third-party source, scored-attempt or study-save changes are needed.
