## Subject conventions

Keep all unknowns real and all arithmetic exact. Identify the signed added
constant separately from the coefficient. Subtracting a negative adds its
opposite. Apply a balancing operation to both complete sides. Nonzero scaling
preserves the solution set; multiplying by zero can erase a restriction.

Use equality and inverse-operation arguments plus substitution, not just the
formula x=(c-b)/a. Explain validity versus achieving a specific goal. These
six choice cards need their own role certificates; the existing linear typed
checker does not automatically judge an authored choices.v1 key.

Stop at real linear equations ax+b=c with nonzero bounded integer a. Do not
expand into quadratics, inequalities, variable denominators or proof grading.
Record no-solution/all-solution cases as future separate families.
