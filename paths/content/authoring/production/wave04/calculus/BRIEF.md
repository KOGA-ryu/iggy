# Calculus: integration by substitution

Read ../BRIEF.md, ../assignments.json and source active_substitution in
../SOURCES.json. Only write your assigned source files/authoring folder and
build/production/wave04/calculus/. This brief and registry are coordinator-owned.
Package prod04_calc_substitution version 1, reading suffix _r,
questions _q01 through _q12. Bind to topic_0063.

Use Active Calculus 5.3 Preview Activity 5.3.1(b,c) and Activities 5.3.2-5.3.4.
At least six distinct prompts/subparts must seed the 12 questions. Adapt
complicated instances to a bounded family when necessary and record precisely
what changed. Do not attempt a general integrator. Finite small integer
coefficients and inner polynomials of degree at most three are a starting
scope; include powers, exponential/trigonometric outer functions and at least
one reciprocal/logarithmic case. A small explicitly checked expression grammar
is appropriate, a copied general parser/CAS is not.

q01-q04 introduce substitution including missing constant factors and negative
inner derivatives; q05-q08 practise nonlinear inner functions and log/domain
conditions; q09-q12 include definite integrals and at least two method choices.
At least two definite problems must transform bounds correctly. Keep domains
within intervals where integrands are defined; no hidden improper integral.

Define integrand, antiderivative, substitution, differential, chain rule,
constant family, and transformed bounds. Show u=g(x), du=g'(x)dx, complete
conversion with any scale factor, integration in u, back-substitution for an
indefinite result, and differentiation against the original integrand. Definite
results use consistently transformed u-bounds or consistent x-antiderivatives,
never mixed coordinates. Check endpoints independently and explain signs.
For log results use ln|g(x)| and an explicit connected domain; explain why the
integration constant is arbitrary. Define any restricted exponent/log rules.

Derive checks from original functions: chain factors, all intermediate forms,
derivatives, constants, domains and definite evaluation. Equivalent constant
families are duplicate answers. Reject missing factors, false back-substitution,
wrong signs, mixed bounds, pole crossings and true-but-wrong-form options;
include valid equivalent controls. Symbolic identities, not samples, establish
antiderivatives. If a source seed is outside the finite verifier, adapt it or
select another approved seed and record the exclusion honestly.
