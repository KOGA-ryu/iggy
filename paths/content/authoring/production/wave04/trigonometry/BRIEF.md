# Trigonometry: equations using identities

Read ../BRIEF.md, ../assignments.json and source precalc_trig in ../SOURCES.json.
Only write your assigned source files/authoring folder and
build/production/wave04/trigonometry/. This brief and registry are coordinator-owned.
Package prod04_trig_identity_equations version 1, reading suffix _r,
questions _q01 through _q12. Bind to topic_0033.

Use Section 7.1 Examples 1-4 and Exercises 13-32, 37-42. Choose or adapt
coefficients to exact solutions with rational multiples of pi. At least six
different prompts/subparts must be represented. Define a finite expression
and exact angle domain; restrict cases to equations whose complete branches
can be proved, not inferred by scanning a finite list of angles.

q01-q04 introduce factoring and quadratic equations in sine/cosine;
q05-q08 require Pythagorean or reciprocal identities, including a rejected
trig-value branch outside [-1,1]; q09-q12 mix these with at least two explicit
method choices and original domain exclusions. Include a no-solution case,
a case whose zero-factor solutions would be lost by division, and a reciprocal
equation retaining every original sine/cosine exclusion. Use [0,2pi) with
explicit radians and endpoint inclusion throughout this first family.

Define identity versus equation, factor, substitution, zero product, inverse
trig value versus complete solution set, period and original domain. Show the
identity conversion, algebraic branches, allowed function values, all angles
on the interval and substitution into the original equation. Do not divide by
sin/cos without retaining zero branches. Show why no other solutions remain.

Checks must cover each option/reached state, identity/domain equivalence,
exact angle values and complete root sets, including multiple branches and
0 versus 2pi. Reject missing branches, invalid reciprocal inputs, lost roots,
principal-value-only sets and reordered/equivalent-angle duplicates. Do not
copy another general symbolic parser or use floating samples as a proof.
