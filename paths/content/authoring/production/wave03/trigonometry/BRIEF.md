questions with suffixes _q01 through _q12. Bind all content to topic_0033 /
Identities. Titles must not reveal an expression's answer or the chosen method.

Build complete simplifications or identity verifications, on their full stated
original common domains. Use rational expressions in sin(theta), cos(theta),
quotient/reciprocal identities and the Pythagorean identity. Bound coefficients,
powers and denominator shapes before writing; optional double-angle identities
must remain inside an explicitly checked finite algebraic representation.
Choose 4 introductory, 4 practice and 4 mixed multi-identity problems. Include
factoring, common denominators and conjugate rationalization. At least two
mixed problems select a strategy and execute it. Do not pad a short identity
with repeated arithmetic to achieve a step count.

Track original exclusions from sine, cosine and 1 plus/minus sine/cosine
denominators before any cancellation. A simplified expression may extend to
more angles, but the identity as originally posed retains its common domain.
Never clear denominators without their nonzero conditions. State a precise
requested form when several valid identities could answer an open-ended
prompt. A mathematically valid alternative that misses that form gets truthful
feedback. Neither titles nor domains should disclose the simplified answer.

Teach the unit-circle relation, identity versus equation, reciprocal and
quotient definitions, common domain, cancellation and difference of squares.
Work one distinct example with every restriction and arithmetic/algebraic line
explained. A final check is an exact identity proof on the retained domain,
not confirmation at a few angles or a claim that symbolic names match.

Certificate tests must prove the finite identities independently of labels and
keys, and check each local form and original domain. Exact polynomial reduction
using s^2+c^2=1 with rational coefficients and explicit denominator conditions
is one bounded approach; reuse valid helpers if present. Do not build a general
CAS or Markdown parser. Include different-spelling equivalent options,
cancelled-domain loss and a true but goal-missing expression as rejected probes,
with equivalent valid controls. Name any unsupported form precisely.
