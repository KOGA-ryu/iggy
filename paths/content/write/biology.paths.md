@lesson document_membrane_reading | The boundary of an animal cell
@template lesson.v1
The cell membrane forms the cell's boundary. It separates the inside from
the surroundings and regulates the passage of substances into and out of
the cell. In an animal cell, this membrane is not a cell wall.
