## Unresolved differences

1. The missing historical profile section means the 25/50/50/50/25 mm zones,
   12 mm crest, and 6 mm hollow must remain replaceable authored values.
2. The package proves an isolated complete order, not its use on the actual
   production portal with measured jambs, capitals, wall transition, and
   game-like scene lighting.
3. The body palette and roughness are artistically calibrated; neither is a
   laboratory reflectance measurement for the Old Sarum stones.
4. No Unreal reconstruction or performance capture exists.

Items 1–4 prevent archaeological, accepted-asset, and engine-parity claims.
They do not invalidate the current Blender production-candidate boundary.
