# v004.1 Construction-Response Sweep Review

## Bounded question

This sweep asked one question: can the rejected shape-normalized broad normal
be replaced by construction-owned surface response without changing v004.1's
colour, roughness, oxide coverage, luster, shader topology, geometry, or
condition?

The four rows were:

1. A — unchanged v004.1;
2. B — one analytic parent-rest-stock field per leaf, carried through the
   rolled knuckles;
3. C — one separately owned axial/circumferential pintle response;
4. D — exact B plus exact C.

The comparison board is
`output/forged_iron_v004_1_construction_response_sweep_v1/forged_iron_v004_1_construction_response_board.png`.
Columns are integrated height, normal angle, close neutral, grazing, pivot
close, and gameplay. Height uses one fixed plus/minus 6 mm display. Normal
angle uses one fixed zero-to-two-degree display, so saturated regions are
failures rather than a per-row contrast adjustment.

## Decision

**Reject all visible height/normal fields. Retain only the corrected coordinate
ownership.**

No row becomes an integrated material. No normal image, pass vocabulary,
height amplitude, or harmonic field advances. The following structural facts
do advance as the next response-routing contract:

- moving leaf and moving knuckles share `moving_leaf_stock`;
- fixed leaf and fixed knuckles share `fixed_leaf_stock`;
- each knuckle samples an unrolled continuation of the parent strap and its
  real axial placement;
- the pintle is separately owned round stock;
- a secondary roll or axial frame may orient a response, but object shape may
  not silently become material identity.

## What the board actually shows

### A — unchanged v004.1 control

A remains the known failure control. Its height and angle bands form one or two
smooth whole-host lobes. Every component restarts the same three-rail,
sixteen-knot recipe, and the optical seed also restarts by component name. The
physical panels are quiet, but they do not communicate leaf stock, rolled eye,
or separate pin manufacture.

### B — parent rest stock

B solves the ownership equation and fails the visible field equation.

The fixed and moving lineages sample separate continuous analytic fields. All
five leaf-to-eye boundary comparisons have exactly `0.0 m` height mismatch.
The response nevertheless closes each compact C2 influence support into a
large amoeba-like lobe in the angle proof. The script never drew an ellipse or
added a dent, but its finite support is still legible as a repeated motif. That
is the same practical failure: the hammer-envelope proxy became a visible
pattern.

The rolled eyes also receive an implausibly uneven hierarchy:

| Component | B peak-to-peak height | Median angle | P95 angle |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fixed leaf | 3.233 mm | 0.361 deg | 1.233 deg |
| Moving leaf | 3.458 mm | 0.266 deg | 1.178 deg |
| Moving knuckle 01 | 0.619 mm | 0.000 deg | 0.356 deg |
| Fixed knuckle 02 | 2.373 mm | 1.025 deg | 1.987 deg |
| Moving knuckle 03 | 0.938 mm | 0.156 deg | 0.764 deg |
| Fixed knuckle 04 | 0.893 mm | 0.158 deg | 0.941 deg |
| Moving knuckle 05 | 0.047 mm | 0.000 deg | 0.057 deg |

Protected rest is necessary, but this field turns a lineage's accidental
support coverage into which eye looks forged and which eye looks untouched.
That is not a construction observation.

B is ranked first only because its coordinate ownership is the useful donor.
Its pixels and visible response are rejected.

### C — separate progressive-round pintle

C correctly assigns the pin a separate owner and then overstates the most
literal part of the forging sequence. A one-millimetre authored height range
wrapped around the `0.163 m` circumferential field yields:

- median normal angle: `3.625 deg`;
- P95 normal angle: `6.735 deg`;
- maximum normal angle: `7.523 deg`.

The weak eight-fold construction ancestor is therefore not weak in normal
space. It approaches explicit octagonal striping, even though the height range
sounds smaller than A. This is why height amplitude alone is a poor authoring
control: frequency and available circumference determine slope.

The pin seam is numerically closed at `0.0 m`. Its circumference-mean height
varies by only `0.00233 mm` across axial stations, so the field does not form
axial rings. Those contracts pass. The visible field still fails.

More importantly, the excessive isolated response barely affects the final
hinge. Full-frame PSNR against A is `70.10 dB` close, `80.43 dB` grazing,
`63.76 dB` at the pivot, and `82.21 dB` at gameplay distance. The pin occupies
little screen area and the rough dielectric oxide suppresses the detail. More
normal strength would make the isolated octagonal failure worse without
creating the intended clean forged-metal read.

### D — combined construction

D is the exact union of B and C and therefore combines both failure modes. It
adds no new evidence. It is ranked last and rejected.

## Causal diagnosis

The sweep disproves the assumption that missing manufacturing identity is
primarily a normal-amplitude problem.

1. Correct rest-stock coordinates are necessary and reusable.
2. A tool-envelope-shaped correlation kernel remains a visible tool stamp if
   the kernel's closed support survives into normal slope.
3. A historically documented intermediate form is not automatically a visible
   final texture. Square-to-octagonal-to-round describes a process; it does not
   prove eight persistent normal ridges.
4. Rough compact oxide can hide a numerically strong normal. A hidden defect is
   not an accepted layer.
5. The user's observed clean-metal uniformity is more plausibly carried by
   direction-dependent reflection, restrained broad roughness organization,
   and actual geometric plane quality than by another field of surface dents.

## Frozen successes

- v004.1 source hash unchanged before and after all twenty-four renders;
- no saved candidate or packed candidate map;
- four independent source reopenings and persistent render data disabled;
- shared response-group topology unchanged;
- base-colour, independent-roughness, and zero-luster source hashes identical
  in every row;
- exact leaf/eye height continuity at each root boundary;
- exact pintle circumference height closure;
- no rust, damage, scratches, dents, polish, exposed conductor, oxide height,
  AI imagery, or Unreal-parity claim.

## Exact next gate

Do not generate another hammer or facet normal pattern.

First decompose the clean manufacturing read into three response owners and
compare them cheaply on the same hinge:

1. **Geometry plane quality** — current broad faces, bevel lands, rolled-eye
   geometry, and pintle roundness under neutral clay and a moving strip. This
   establishes which missing highlight changes cannot be texture-owned.
2. **Broad roughness organization** — a quiet, open, non-compact field in the
   accepted parent-rest-stock coordinates, with no height source and no copied
   grayscale from colour. It may change the width and continuity of the
   dielectric highlight without drawing a mark.
3. **Direction-dependent reflection** — a bounded construction tangent and
   anisotropic response tested separately for flat strap, rolled eye, and pin.
   It may orient reflection but may not draw brushing lines, grooves, damage,
   or a fixed light direction.

Use A as the frozen v004.1 control, then isolate roughness-only,
direction-response-only, and their combination. Preserve the current no-luster
candidate until that exploratory response-routing board demonstrates that a
new shader lane belongs. Any topology change remains disposable until selected
and separately frozen beneath a new coded integration demand.
