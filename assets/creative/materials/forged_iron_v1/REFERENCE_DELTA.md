# Forged-Iron Reference Delta

## Decision

This candidate is a clean intact hot-worked architectural-iron core, not an
aged door photograph and not a universal “old metal” material. It is judged on
the approved openwork hinge. Geometry supplies the functional outline,
apertures, leaf thickness, knuckle alternation, pintle, and bevel lands. The
material supplies compact forge skin, broad temperature/scale colour, finite
tool-plane response, restrained worked direction, and close micro-response.

## Causal layer stack

1. **Geometry construction** — eight original hinge meshes remain the owner of
   silhouette, openwork, holes, bevel lands, barrel segmentation, and pintle.
2. **Macro compact-scale colour** — sixteen coordinated dark shades are blended
   through ten finite broad fields inside each component.
3. **Scale plate tone** — finite overlapping regions change scale tone,
   roughness, sub-20-micrometre lip height, and normal without exposing metal.
4. **Finite tool planes** — planish and cross-peen events alter slope, signed
   macro height, and roughness. They never change metalness or base colour.
5. **Worked-face response** — finite local passes alter tangent normal,
   roughness, anisotropy, and rotation, with protected quiet rest.
6. **Micro surface** — the 6.4 micrometre modern hot-forged proxy is subordinate
   close response and disappears with distance.
7. **Optional conductor** — a valid bare-iron Principled branch exists for a
   future contact mask, but the default mask and compiled metalness are zero.
8. **Narrative state** — rust, damage, soot, dirt, blood, oil, and contact wear
   remain separate placement-dependent capabilities.

## Repair record

### Repair 1 — physical-layer error

The earlier generator converted strong hammer activity into bright conductive
islands. That produced rounded silver rectangles and confused tool action with
material removal. The conductor mask is now identically zero. Hammer events
survive only through their causal normal, height, and roughness responses.

### Repair 2 — scale-coupling and proof error

The first giant-hinge proof enlarged every surface band and was over-lit almost
white. Fabrication scaling now affects macro tool coordinates and macro height
only. Scale colour, oxide-lip, worked, and micro lanes retain world-metre scale.
The proof light was recalibrated to retain dark value groups.

### Repair 3 — target-framing error

The first acceptance frame showed only the moving leaf and hid the pointed end
and fixed leaf. The renderer now computes bounds from every target object in
world space and sizes the orthographic frame from the complete 4.06 m assembly.
This makes the proof capable of revealing material behavior across all eight
functional pieces.

### Repair 4 — repeated worked rows

The high-resolution worked-response diagnostic exposed a grid caused by using
the 1.218 by 0.164 m scale tile for local finishing. Worked response now owns a
2.436 by 0.492 m raw-metre coordinate, and twelve irregular passes replace the
alternating two-row layout. Millimetre bundle rhythm is preserved; only the
composition period becomes large enough to avoid obvious rows on 0.41 m stock.

## Honest unresolved differences

- The candidate has no authored usage history. Contact polish, bearing wear,
  oxidation, and damage require geometry-aware placement in later passes.
- The directional worked-response diagnostic is deliberately loud and reveals
  its finite pass boundaries. The combined material uses them at restrained
  normal, roughness, and anisotropy strengths.
- The giant fabrication multiplier is an art-direction rule for the giant
  house, not a historical claim.
- The present target is evaluated in Blender. Unreal coordinate, tangent,
  roughness, normal-combination, displacement, and distance parity are false
  until a side-by-side engine proof exists.
- No material pass can repair weak leaf taper, aperture relief, bevel
  proportion, or knuckle clearance; those remain geometry acceptance concerns.

## Candidate threshold

The candidate may advance to human visual review only when:

- all eight target objects reopen with the canonical material;
- metalness is exactly zero and no hammer-derived exposure exists;
- all twelve authored image lanes are packed and live;
- macro and surface coordinates remain separated;
- the complete hinge appears in the neutral and material front proofs;
- grazing light reveals finite planes without melting the flat bar;
- base colour contains multiple blended dark shades inside one leaf;
- damage and corrosion terms are absent from the live core;
- focused profile, generator, saved-blend, and workflow-entry gates are green.
