# Forged Iron v004.2 Integration Review

## Decision

**Prefer v004.2 as the forged-iron repair candidate. Do not accept or register
it as a donor.**

The reviewed board is
`output/forged_iron_connected_oxide_candidate_v004_2/cycles_v004_1_v004_2_proof_v1/forged_iron_v004_1_v004_2_cycles_comparison.png`.
Row A is saved v004.1. Row B is saved and separately reopened v004.2.

## Strongest visible achievement

The selected response behaves like an optical property rather than another
painted pattern. It changes highlight travel on the long leaf, rolled eyes,
and pin while leaving broad quiet fields intact. Neither opposed moving-strip
view produces brushing lines, grooves, repeated symbols, cat-face/diamond
pareidolia, tangent bands, or a fixed light direction.

The change is balanced and reproduces the disposable-C selection exactly:

| Physical panel | v004.2 against v004.1 PSNR |
| --- | ---: |
| close neutral | 44.030040 dB |
| strip left | 39.282581 dB |
| strip right | 39.474193 dB |
| pivot close | 44.397690 dB |
| gameplay | 49.521032 dB |

The live direction-amount proof moves from zero to a uniform `0.28`. All eight
base-colour, roughness, and source-normal float hashes remain identical, and
the two decoded source-normal proof tiles are pixel-identical. The different
PNG container hashes are only independent encodings of those same pixels.

## Weakest visible area

The improvement is deliberately subordinate and becomes subtle at gameplay
distance. It makes the clean intact oxide respond more plausibly; it does not
create a complete forged-surface identity by itself.

More importantly, this build inherits the old broad-normal field only to keep
the response experiment isolated. The construction audit already rejected
that shape-normalized owner, and this proof does not rehabilitate it. The
pivot also remains a smooth coated cylinder whose bearing gaps and highlight
continuity are geometry-owned. v004.2 neither repairs nor disguises that
construction limit.

## Rejection audit

- Tangent seams: none visible on leaf, rolled eye, or pin.
- Brushing lines or grooves: absent.
- Narrow plastic highlights: absent; the response stays broad under both
  opposed strips.
- Baked light direction: absent; left and right strip reversals both behave.
- Frozen image drift: absent across all eight components.
- Gameplay noise: absent.
- Condition, rust, damage, contact polish, or exposed conductor: absent.
- Claim to repair pivot geometry: explicitly rejected.

Normalized difference diagnostics show broad redistribution around real
silhouettes, apertures, joins, and cylindrical curvature. They do not reveal
a compact support, repeated finite motif, or tangent-aligned stripe.

## Acceptance boundary

This is one repair pass after v004.1. The saved candidate, separate-process
reopen, and paired Cycles proof pass their bounded contracts. Manual material
acceptance, professional-reference matched comparison, donor registration,
and Unreal parity are not established.

The next material workstream should remove or replace the rejected broad-normal
owner as its own isolated decision. It must compare geometry normals alone
against the frozen v004.2 response before authoring any new normal field.
