## Why this consumer is first

The arming sword is the library flagship and the current material is the
largest known visual defect. Its straight local frame exercises the accepted
longitudinal steel response without adding curved-blade coordinate work. Its
lenticular body, secondary bevels, edge land, ricasso cap, and tip are enough
to test semantic region ownership. Its Soldier, Knight, and Lord tiers later
exercise bright steel, selective bluing, and decoration without requiring
three unrelated base materials.

No fuller or construction-specific pattern is enabled. A dark or pale stripe
in the old render is not evidence of a fuller, pattern welding, a hamon, or
lamination.

## Mesh-intake gate

The source-intake gate is complete:

1. the worker builds in metres with Blender 5.1.1;
2. the blade is a distinct `SW_blade.001` object; the earlier empty
   `SW_blade` placeholder must never be selected by first-name matching;
3. the evaluated blade dimensions and all-quad topology are recorded above;
4. the source has no UV layer or material before its finish script;
5. `zone_id`, `slot`, and `sharp_face` attributes survive on the blade, but the
   zone label is not trusted as the side-face semantic owner because the face
   audit exposed the misplaced edge strip;
6. normalized cross-section position owns side-face body, bevel, and edge;
   face orientation and longitudinal position own the shoulder and tip caps;
   source zone labels remain audit evidence only;
7. no modifier is required to create the blade form; the finish script later
   adds palette colour, roughness 0.8, smooth shading, and an outline modifier;
8. integration must add `IGGY_BladeUV` and `sinc_sword_region` without
   replacing source geometry.

The first integration may not rename, reshape, triangulate, rebake, or replace
the production mesh. It creates a generated copy, installs the clean-steel
material, adapts only the coordinate/semantic boundary, and emits before/after
proofs.
