# Prompt 03: Select One Blade-Identity Fork

## Objective

Add exactly one construction-specific blade identity over the accepted clean
foundation. Select `none` when the evidence supports homogeneous steel.

## Fork A: homogeneous European or fantasy steel

No additional material pattern. Preserve the clean substrate. Optional maker
marks and finish transitions belong to Prompt 04.

## Fork B: Japanese differential polish

Required semantics may include `shinogi_ji`, `ji_hira`, hardened edge, hamon,
kissaki, yokote, and boshi.

Two strategies:

1. hand-authored spline with variable width, softness, interruption, and
   separate face corrections;
2. edge-distance field modulated by authored control points and then corrected
   by deterministic subtractive paint.

The hamon affects supported roughness, value, and scattering relationships. It
is not a white decal. Sashikomi and hadori are different finish presets and may
not be blended accidentally.

## Fork C: hada or laminated blade skin

Use a low-contrast forged-flow field aligned to the actual folding and grind.
It must remain subordinate to the polish and vary coherently across both faces.
Do not use generic marble noise.

## Fork D: pattern-welded blade

Two strategies:

1. construct billet layers, apply declared twist/stack/weld operations, and
   mathematically expose them through the blade grind;
2. use a licensed or internally authored pattern reference registered to the
   blade and constrained by construction masks.

Pattern welding and crucible/watered steel are separate families. Reject any
route that uses one wavy texture for both.

## Fork E: hammered or forge-finished blade

Geometry or high-to-low transfer owns real depressions. The mask vocabulary
must use finite impacts with pressure, direction, overlap, protected edge, and
quiet zones. Voronoi cells are not hammer blows.

## Fork F: fictional energy, coating, or magical material

Define the physical substrate, coating, emissive source, animation coordinate,
and gameplay purpose separately. Do not use magic to conceal an unproved steel
foundation.

## Exit gate

Only one fork enters the champion material. If construction remains ambiguous,
select `none` and preserve the clean blade.
