# Prompt 05: Condition and Use History — Optional

## Default decision

Condition is disabled. Record a verified skip unless the active capability
explicitly names use history, maintenance, corrosion, or damage.

## When condition is authorized

Split it into independent families:

- maintenance haze or oil film;
- scabbard draw/contact polish;
- handling residue near guard and ricasso;
- longitudinal scabbard scratches;
- sharpening history at the cutting edge;
- impact notches or rolls at plausible contact positions;
- stable dark patina;
- active red oxidation;
- deep corrosion and material loss;
- dirt, blood, soot, or magical residue as scene overlays.

For every family state the event, eligible region, protected region,
orientation, size, density, age, material lanes, and geometry threshold.

## Placement logic

- Scabbard marks follow insertion and withdrawal.
- Sharpening marks remain tied to the edge and its service history.
- Impact damage requires a plausible contact event and cannot cover every edge.
- Corrosion follows storage, moisture retention, coatings, and exposed metal.
- Deep pits and missing edge material belong to geometry or high-to-low
  transfer.

## Rejection rules

- No universal smart-material edge wear.
- No evenly sprayed scratches.
- No rust over polished intact conductor without an exposure history.
- No damage used to make a weak clean material interesting.
- No same mask copied into colour, roughness, normal, and metalness.

## Exit gate

If condition is outside scope, tests must prove all condition masks, shader
switches, and manifest claims are absent or zero. If enabled, each family is a
separate capability with its own proof and repair count.
