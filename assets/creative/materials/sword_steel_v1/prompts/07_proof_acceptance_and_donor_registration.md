# Prompt 07: Proof, Acceptance, and Donor Registration

## Objective

Try to disprove the sword material on the actual consumer, record its visible
shortcomings, and register reusable components only after they survive.

## Required proof set

1. clay front and three-quarter geometry;
2. neutral physical material;
3. grazing light;
4. controlled light or object rotation sequence;
5. gameplay distance and thumbnail;
6. first and second blade faces;
7. tip, ricasso, fuller, bevel, and edge close-ups;
8. semantic-region isolation;
9. macro-polish isolation;
10. medium-grind isolation;
11. selected identity-fork isolation;
12. roughness, normal, metalness, and optional stylization channels;
13. engine proof when parity is claimed;
14. saved-file reopen validation.

Use fixed cameras, exposure, lights, and colour management for comparisons.
Lighting must show a range of values without flattening the blade into a white
bar.

## Defect ledger

Critique the entire proof before editing:

```text
geometry_and_silhouette:
plane_and_edge_read:
conductor_response:
macro_polish:
medium_grind:
anisotropic_microresponse:
identity_pattern:
symbols_or_pareidolia:
repetition_or_aliasing:
distance_read:
both_face_continuity:
host_asset_integration:
engine_parity:
```

Bundle related failures beneath the highest causal owner. Run one changed
proof while repairing and regenerate the full set once the batch converges.

## Acceptance statuses

- `diagnostic-candidate`: isolated fixture only.
- `production-candidate`: built, audited, packed, and reopened on the scoped
  consumer but awaiting review.
- `accepted`: actual sword proof, explicit review, and all required gates
  green.

## Donor registration

Register individual accepted components, not only the full material:

- conductor substrate;
- blade semantic schema;
- macro polish builder;
- finite grind builder;
- anisotropic microresponse;
- hamon, hada, pattern-weld, engraving, and condition components when later
  accepted;
- Blender and engine adapters.

## Exit gate

State the strongest read, weakest read, repair count, remaining difference
from the professional references, actual-target status, and engine-parity
status before acceptance.
