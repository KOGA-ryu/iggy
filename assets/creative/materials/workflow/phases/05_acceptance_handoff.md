Execute `workflow/prompts/10_final_validation_and_handoff.md`.

## Candidate versus accepted

`production-candidate` means the scoped system is built, proved, reopened, and
ready for review.

`accepted` additionally requires:

- `hero-master`: proof on the named actual target asset, under neutral and
  game-like lighting, plus explicit user acceptance;
- `reusable-family`: proof on at least one real consumer and explicit user or
  owning-art-director acceptance;
- `bounded-variation`: proof on the inherited consumer and green parent
  invariants.

An isolated fixture can establish a production candidate. It cannot establish
hero acceptance.

## Final gates

- complete build to canonical output;
- all focused and reopen tests;
- full manifest and packed-file hashes;
- performance budgets;
- reference-delta status;
- stale-output and dormant-route audit;
- coded-demand drift check;
- actual-target proof policy;
- exact excluded lanes;
- final `WORKSTREAM.md`;
- uncommitted or committed state reported truthfully.

Only after those gates may the active goal be completed.
