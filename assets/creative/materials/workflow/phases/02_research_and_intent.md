# Phase 2: Research and Intent

Execute:

1. `workflow/prompts/02_reference_research.md`
2. `workflow/prompts/03_research_to_intent.md`

The tier controls breadth, never evidence quality.

## Outcomes

- written reference ledger;
- image-to-text translations where images are used;
- professional comparison and explicit remaining delta;
- measured, proxy, authored, inherited, and unsupported claims separated;
- geometry-versus-texture-versus-shader ownership;
- macro, medium, edge, event, and micro anatomy;
- machine-auditable layer provenance from source through output, consumer,
  isolated proof, and rest rule;
- ordered surface-method contract with exact composition, masks, coordinates,
  geometry-to-map transfer, and damage-placement state;
- layered color order;
- independent height, normal, roughness, AO, metalness, and opacity causes;
- exact coordinate and distance contracts;
- `REFERENCE_DELTA.md`;
- updated profile and `RESEARCH_INTENT.md`.

## Reference delta requirement

Every `hero-master` and `reusable-family` package must create
`REFERENCE_DELTA.md`. For each professional benchmark, record:

- construction or silhouette feature;
- value and color hierarchy;
- material response;
- repetition strategy;
- what the candidate implements;
- what remains visibly different;
- whether the remaining difference blocks candidate or acceptance status.

The file is not a praise sheet. At least one unresolved visual difference must
remain until a proof genuinely closes it.

## Hard exit gate

Do not enter Phase 3 while an implementation-changing dimension, layer, or
coordinate rule is merely implied by a reference image.
