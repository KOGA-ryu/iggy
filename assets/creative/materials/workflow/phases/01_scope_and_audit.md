# Phase 1: Scope and Audit

This is the first of five visible material-workflow phases.

Execute the detailed prompts:

1. `workflow/prompts/00_goal_bootstrap.md`
2. `workflow/prompts/01_existing_asset_audit.md`

## Outcomes

- one canonical package;
- one visible capability;
- one actual target asset or explicit isolated-candidate boundary;
- one workflow tier;
- exact included and excluded lanes;
- normalized substrate, construction, finish, condition, stylization, and
  renderer composition from the texture workbench;
- at-hand shared tools, package donors, missing tools, and rejected routes;
- at least two strategies for every unresolved major component;
- the first failing observable;
- current dirty-state ownership;
- a populated `WORKSTREAM.md`.

## Choose the workflow tier

### `hero-master`

Use for a hero asset, reusable master material, new construction vocabulary,
or system expected to define quality for later assets.

Required:

- full written research and professional comparison;
- measured-versus-authored claim ledger;
- complete coded demands;
- neutral, live, close, grazing, distance, mask, topology, and actual-target
  proofs;
- performance budget;
- saved-file reopen validation;
- user review before `accepted`.

### `reusable-family`

Use for a reusable material family derived from an accepted construction and
surface vocabulary.

Required:

- source audit focused on changed anatomy;
- inherited-contract ledger;
- complete coded demands for changed routes;
- neutral, live, distance, and one real-consumer proof;
- performance budget;
- saved-file reopen validation.

### `bounded-variation`

Use for a colorway, age preset, scale preset, packed-channel change, or other
variation whose parent construction and shader are already accepted.

Required:

- exact parent package and inherited schema;
- changed-value and changed-route inventory only;
- focused tests;
- one isolated before/after proof and one consumer proof;
- parent performance budget must remain green.

It may not introduce new geometry, material science, damage anatomy, shader
topology, or coordinate logic. If it does, promote it to `reusable-family`.

## Hard exit gate

Do not enter Phase 2 until `WORKSTREAM.md` names the tier, normalized material
composition, at-hand tool set, strategy alternatives, and first failing
observable. “Make it better” is not a capability, and “start coding then look
for tools as needed” is not an authored workflow.
