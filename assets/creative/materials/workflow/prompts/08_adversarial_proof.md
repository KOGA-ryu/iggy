# Prompt 08: Produce Adversarial Proof and Professional Comparison

Judge the rendered surface, not the effort.

## Required reading

- package `WORKSTREAM.md`;
- package `CODED_DEMANDS.md`;
- `workflow/07_proof_and_acceptance/AGENTS.md`;
- professional benchmark sources from Stage 02;
- final texture and Blender manifests.

## Apply the tier proof burden

Read `workflow_contract.tier` from the profile and use its minimum proof
burden:

- `hero-master`: all six proof groups below, actual-target proof, performance
  audit, and explicit manual review;
- `reusable-family`: consumer-asset proof plus every proof group affected by
  the family contract; unchanged hero-only views may be inherited by exact
  hash;
- `bounded-variation`: changed-property proofs, one representative consumer,
  deterministic/manifest checks, and inherited family evidence by exact hash.

Never reduce the proof burden because rendering is slow. Use the builder's
`none` or `changed` mode during repairs, then produce the tier-required final
set. Inherited evidence must name the source package and artifact hash.

## Freeze candidate

Confirm:

- the Phase 3 board selected exactly one champion and recorded every rejected
  candidate reason;
- champion seed and profile version;
- final map resolution;
- final Blender material version;
- packed images;
- saved-file hash;
- focused generator, contract, and reopen gates green.

If any changed, rebuild before proof.

The Phase 3 reduced-resolution comparison board is selection evidence, not
this final proof. Do not promote its renders to full-resolution acceptance
evidence, and do not rebuild discarded candidates at final resolution.

## Produce six proof groups

All six are mandatory for `hero-master`. For lower tiers, record each group as
`executed`, `inherited-by-hash`, or `not-applicable` under the tier rule above.

### 1. Matched benchmark

Compare with at least two professional references at approximately matched crop
and light. Annotate at least five concrete gaps.

### 2. Geometry and form under light

Show neutral geometry, relevant orthographic or side/end views, frontal light,
three-quarter light, grazing light, and height/normal isolation.

### 3. Frequency and causality

Show pattern, macro, medium, edges/events, micro, cumulative height, final
normal, and final response with physical spans. Each isolated proof must name
the causal surface component, eligible region, protected rest region, local
frame, and composition operation.

### 4. Color and stylization

Show unlit base color, cumulative color layers, one element enlarged,
desaturated value grouping, gameplay thumbnail, ink, highlight, direction, and
detail priority.

### 5. Reflection and packing

Show roughness highlight behavior, AO, physical metal/dielectric layers,
normal-band combination, height range, and packed channels.

### 6. Repetition and world use

Show 1x, 4x, 16x; multiple variants; representative mips; close, gameplay,
distant; corner or curved transition; and actual target asset.

## Inspect visually

Open the proof images. Do not infer appearance from metrics.

Record:

- strongest visible result;
- weakest visible result;
- obvious procedural signature;
- repeated landmarks;
- linework quality;
- within-element shade quality;
- roughness story;
- close, gameplay, and distance behavior;
- actual-asset behavior;
- remaining professional-reference gaps.

Explicitly inspect the decisive finite masks and 3x3-or-larger repetition
proof for faces, animals, diamonds, symbols, stripes, grid rhythms, or repeated
silhouettes. Subtlety in the beauty render does not excuse a failed isolated
mask.

## Decision

Choose:

- `repair-required`;
- `production-candidate`;
- `rejected`.

Do not choose `accepted` without the required manual authority.

If any proof group is missing or flattering rather than adversarial, choose
`repair-required`.

An isolated fixture may reach `production-candidate`; it cannot satisfy a
tier-required actual-target proof. The target must be the real consumer asset,
with its real dimensions, modifiers, joins, coordinate attributes, and
game-like lighting.
