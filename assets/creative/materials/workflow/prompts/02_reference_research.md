# Prompt 02: Research the Physical Material and Professional Solutions

Execute research for the exact capability and visible failure recorded in the
dossier.

## Required reading

- package `WORKSTREAM.md`;
- `workflow/01_reference_research/AGENTS.md`;
- current package source ledger and `RESEARCH_INTENT.md`;
- professional comparison section of `QUALITY_BENCHMARK.md`.

## Research method

1. Search written sources first.
2. Find physical construction or material-anatomy authority.
3. Find real measurements for the important visible scales.
4. Find official written Blender or engine implementation guidance.
5. Find at least two high-quality assets or shipped-use examples with the same
   material role.
6. If a useful source is a video, obtain and read its transcript.
7. If a useful source is an image, translate it into written observations,
   geometry, palettes, measurements, ambiguity notes, and implementation
   consequences.
8. Record URLs, titles, dates, licenses, exact values, and transfer limits.

## Research depth

Cover the relevant subset:

- how the material forms;
- internal anatomy or layer stack;
- direction and local frame;
- construction pattern;
- macro, medium, edge, and micro structures;
- feature dimensions, spacing, density, and depth;
- color families and within-element shade movement;
- roughness and reflection behavior;
- material response under cutting, working, or fabrication;
- how high-quality artists simplify the material;
- how production assets combine tileables, trims, atlases, decals, and
  overlays;
- what belongs in geometry, texture, shader, or scene context.

## Professional comparison

For each selected benchmark, record in the dossier:

- construction and silhouette;
- macro value grouping;
- medium material identity;
- edge treatment;
- color layering;
- reflection;
- stylization;
- repetition strategy;
- actual world use.

Then list at least five candidate gaps. Every gap must imply a possible
authoring or integration decision.

## Measurement discipline

Classify each value as surveyed, catalogued, laboratory characterized,
technical guidance, qualitative authority, comparative finish, proxy,
authored translation, or unknown.

Do not:

- infer depth from darkness;
- infer color from shadow;
- infer material from an AI caption;
- turn a related-stone proxy into the exact stone;
- treat an artist's taste as physical measurement;
- use a measurement without recording its specimen and transfer scope.

## Artifacts

Update or create the package's relevant reference ledgers. Update the dossier:

- source and measurement ledger;
- professional comparison;
- implementation research notes;
- unknowns and prohibited inferences;
- exact citations supporting the first repair.

Do not yet write the generator or shader repair.

## Exit gate

Do not advance unless:

- the physical construction and material anatomy are understood;
- important visible features have scale evidence or explicit unknown status;
- at least two professional benchmarks are compared in detail;
- a written Blender or engine method exists;
- image and video evidence have been translated into text;
- licenses and source limits are recorded;
- research tells the next stage what to author and what not to invent.

Set Stage 02 to `complete`, then execute `03_research_to_intent.md`.
